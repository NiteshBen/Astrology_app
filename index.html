    Reading your Kundli

    ☉

    Ascendant

    Also called Lagna, the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at your birth. It determines where the houses begin.

    ☾

    Moon sign

    Also called Chandra Rashi, the sidereal zodiac sign occupied by the Moon at your birth.

    ✧

    Nakshatra

    One of 27 divisions of the zodiac. Your birth nakshatra describes the Moon's position in this finer division.

    How is the chart calculated?

    Tara Ved uses geocentric positions, a Lahiri sidereal offset, mean lunar nodes and whole-sign houses. Birthplace supplies the coordinates and time zone. An unknown time uses local noon for estimated planetary positions and leaves out the ascendant and houses.
