    How are these timings calculated?

    Tithi is based on the angle between the Moon and Sun. Sunrise and sunset use the selected city centre and a standard, unobstructed horizon. Rahu Kaal divides daylight into eight intervals and uses the weekday to pick one. Brahma Muhurat uses 96 to 48 minutes before sunrise. Abhijit Muhurat is the middle fifteenth of daylight and is omitted on Wednesday under the convention used here.
